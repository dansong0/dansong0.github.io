I think it was Jung that said the masculine is like a boat, and the feminine is like the ocean.  I think in the same way, the feminine is like the subconscious, and the masculine is the conscious.  The subconscious is significantly more powerful and endlessly bountiful, while the conscious can be directed to great effect, but limited in resources.  It's an age of the masculine - the conscious.  But not conscious in the sense of awareness.  We are conscious of our capacity to think and solve problems, but disconnected from the compelling force that shapes that action.

When people are unconscientious, their attempt to ignore their own conscience causes it to manifest physically.  They project it onto people who behave conscientiously.  That is why you see, for example, people who despise Greta Thunberg.  They hope to in one motion smite her, and their conscience.  But it's futile - the unconscious/conscience has no body that can be destroyed.  It will inevitably take shape again, and the ignored internal battle will become an external one once again.

Anyways, the masculine.  It's the rational to the feminine irrational.  But today the rational, the masculine, is so dominant even girlbosses can only speak the language of the rational.  There's a kind of violence to rationality.  If you can't abide by its rules, there's no place for you.  But very plainly the world runs on irrationality.  There is nothing inherently bad about being irrational, just like there is nothing good about being rational.  When people think of irrational people, they think of insane people or psychopaths.  But when people think of rational people they don't think of bombmakers or financiers.  They think of chemists and engineers.  Why don't we think of dancers and artists when we think of irrational people?

Men have a general proclivity towards the masculine, and women towards the feminine, but each embodies both.  By ignoring or demonizing the subconscious, and only respecting what can be seen, heard, or conceptualized, you will inevitably manifest actual demons.  Men who are violent towards women reject their own feminine, and attempt to destroy women and their own femininity in one motion.  Women who despise men reject their masculine, and attempt to destroy men and their own masculinity.  Very often these people make demons of each other.
