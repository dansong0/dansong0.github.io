title: Ambition
excerpt: Modern ambition is usually about money and status. Doing nothing, if it's what you actually want, can be the more ambitious path.
---

Modern ambition is numerical; growth-oriented.  Ambition is almost wholly associated with money, status, creation, empire.  Ambition is an expression of lofty goals, but what of individual direction?  I think the latter is often forgotten.  I think, doing nothing in this day and age, is ambitious and aspirational.  Have you tried it?  On paper, it is easy, but actually quite difficult in practice.  Sustaining yourself is a bit harder obviously.  There's friends and pests in your ear asking, "what are you going to do?".  You will undoubtedly question yourself.  But if it's what you want, even for just a time, it is an ambitious task.

Conversely, do you really want to get rich?  Or do you just want the comfort that money provides?  Do you really want to be the best?  Or do you just want the safety offered by the broad respect of the people?  Anything conditional is not real.  Furthermore, these ambitions are secondary.  They are not intrinsic to you.  They are a manifestation of your conscious or unconscious fears.  Conscripted soldiers to battle your psychic phantoms.  

Know thyself.  Ambition without direction turns you into a vicious beast.  The collar is a sign of civility, but also a lack of agency.  Anything true to yourself will be difficult; ambitious - it's a path unworn by any other.  But it is also the easiest.  Like lightning flashing through the sky: despite its power, it still takes the path of least resistance.
