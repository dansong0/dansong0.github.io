I was always a pretty sensitive kid.  I figured out pretty quickly that the best way to "toughen up" was to sever my connection to things.  Something happens I don't like?  I don't care.  Someone seem to like me?  Well I'm definitely not going to do anything about it.  And then I go home and fantasize about being in love, being cared for, and so on.  So it was pretty apparent to me that this wasn't a winning strategy.  But it was a dilemma.  Most situations disturbed me such that separating myself from it was easier than seeing it through.

In terms of dealing with my heart, I'm in the stone age.  I suspect we all are.  Banging together sticks and stones.  For me, that severance was important.  I needed to find a way to nourish myself without facing the (many) things that disturbed me.  But after stepping back into it, I realize there's no replacement for firsthand experience.  Just make the call, say the words to the stranger, laugh out loud in public.  It's going to be ugly.  And from my experience, ugly is just the start of it.  I stutter, mutter, and come across completely wrong.  I worry about what the other person thinks.  And it's all quite disturbing.  And I'm still disturbed, just as I ever was.

But I've gotten good at doing those things now.  I think I'm quite good, even.  But having those bad experiences, it's still uncomfortable.  Like being antagonized by a stranger for things outside of your control.  Yeesh.  I have a recent event that immediately comes to mind when I write it.  A couple, actually.  But what changed is now, I can stomach the feeling.  Not reject it, or separate myself from it, like I used to and many others suggest.  I can hold it (aside: I refuse to ever use the phrase "hold space").  Because I'm big now.  Not just in age.  Plenty of geriatric children roam the streets.

There's a lot of ways to get big.  You can allow yourself to take up space.  You can feed and nourish yourself.  You can hit the weight room.  You can be there for yourself.  You can do the things that make you happy and full.

And there's a lot of ways to stay small.  You can confine yourself to someone else's ideals.  You can tie yourself to a person that isn't keen on changing.  You can refuse to do things that are uncomfortable.

The main point I want to make is that it is not a bad thing that your problem does not go away.  It's a pointer that something is as big as you are.  Sometimes that thing is swollen and needs some attention.  Sometimes the answer is to grow yourself.  But the thing will usually still be there.  If you don't get what you want, it's no different if you're a kid or an adult.  It's still not ideal.  But the difference is now you don't throw a tantrum about it.  And I believe it's because you're Big.  After all the problem wasn't its presence - it's that it disturbed you.

The L is holdable.  A large surface disperses heat better.  Losing $100 is not so bad when you have the bag.  Everything is a small setback if it doesn't change your direction.  So get Big!!
